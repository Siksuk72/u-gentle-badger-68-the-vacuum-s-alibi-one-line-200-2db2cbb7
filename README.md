One line, in a robot vacuum's own deadpan voice, for The Vacuum's Alibi
on make.fun:

> Log 0912: spot behind the sofa skipped. Lidar classified the gap as a structural void, and I do not vacuum load-bearing darkness.

## Why it wins

Three beats, no filler. A machine-log setup ("Log 0912: spot behind
the sofa skipped") — a status report, not an apology. A specific sensor
reading (lidar, the gap behind the sofa) — the machine a little smug
about its sensors, exactly as the judge brief asks. And the twist at the
very end: the darkness behind the sofa is *load-bearing*, so the vacuum
treats skipping it as routine policy. The setup and the payoff are the
same gap, the joke never explains itself, and at 129 characters (129
bytes UTF-8) the line is short as well as tight — the tie-break favours
fewer bytes.

## Runners-up (drafted, then cut)

- "The crumbs under the table were not missed. Infrared classified the cluster as a family, and I do not break up families." — Lost: the "family" twist is cute rather than deadpan; the judge brief warns against cute observations about dust.
- "I did not miss the spot by the fridge. My map shows it as a sovereign zone, and I respect international borders." — Lost: strong twist, but "sovereign zone" is a claim about the map, not a sensor reading; less machine-voice than lidar.
- "Spot under the bed: skipped. Cliff sensor read a 2 mm drop, and per protocol I do not enter the void." — Lost: "the void" lands, but "enter the void" is a stock appliance joke by now; "load-bearing darkness" is the fresher specific detail.
- "Spot missed: the rug's fringe tangled my brush, and after 47 seconds of negotiation I accepted its terms." — Lost: funny, but the vacuum negotiating is anthropomorphic pleading in disguise; the winner stays a machine filing a report.
- "The corner by the door remains dirty. My bumper met a wall that was not on the map, so I filed it as a new roommate and gave it space." — Lost: "new roommate" is charming but wordy (134 chars) and the punch is diluted across two clauses.
- "Log 447: the dust under the rug has been there longer than I have. Seniority rules; I clean around it." — Lost: "seniority rules" is a shrug, not a surprise; no specific sensor or twist at the end.
